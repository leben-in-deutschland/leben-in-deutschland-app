Leben in Deutschland – Practice for the Einbürgerungstest: all official questions, offline and free

DESCRIPTION
Leben in Deutschland helps you prepare for the Einbürgerungstest: all 300 official questions plus the 10 for your federal state, a mock exam like the real one (33 questions, 60 minutes), exam readiness, streak and daily goal at a glance, and translations in 7 languages – offline, private and free.

Download for free: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.lebenindeutschland.app
Practice in the browser: https://lebenindeutschland.org

Note: This is not an official Einbürgerungstest and not an official app of the Federal Office for Migration and Refugees (BAMF). The Einbürgerungstest can only be carried out at the BAMF.
