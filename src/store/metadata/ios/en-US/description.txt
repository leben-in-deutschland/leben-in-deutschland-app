Prepare for the Einbürgerungstest “Leben in Deutschland” with every official question, a mock exam that feels like the real one, and a clear view of how ready you really are.

ALL OFFICIAL QUESTIONS
• The 300 official questions of the Einbürgerungstest plus the 10 questions of your federal state (all 16 states)
• Instant feedback on every answer with an easy-to-understand explanation
• Translations in 7 languages: English, Turkish, Russian, French, Arabic, Ukrainian and Hindi
• Have questions read aloud

A MOCK EXAM LIKE THE REAL ONE
• 33 questions, 60 minutes – exactly like the exam
• A clear result at the end: you pass with 17 correct answers
• Your results stay saved so you can see your progress

SEE HOW READY YOU ARE
• Exam readiness based on what you know and how your mock exams went
• Progress per topic, so you know where to practise next
• Enter your exam date and see how many days are left

LEARN THE WAY THAT SUITS YOU
• Learn new questions, review what is due, fix your mistakes, revisit flagged questions
• A quick round of 10 questions or a longer session – you decide
• Reviews are scheduled automatically so that answers stick

STAY ON TRACK – EVERY DAY
• Streak, weekly overview and a daily goal keep you going
• An optional reminder at your study time
• Milestones and achievements for your progress

OFFLINE, PRIVATE, FREE
• Works without internet – ideal for the bus or train
• No account needed, no ads, no tracking
• Your learning progress stays on your device
• Light and dark mode, larger text available
• Open source – the code is public

The app is free. If it helps you, you can leave a small optional tip – no hidden features, no subscriptions.

Good luck with your exam!

NOTE
This is not an official Einbürgerungstest. This app is designed to help you prepare for the Einbürgerungstest. It is a private offering and not an official app of the Federal Office for Migration and Refugees (BAMF). The Einbürgerungstest can only be carried out at the BAMF.
