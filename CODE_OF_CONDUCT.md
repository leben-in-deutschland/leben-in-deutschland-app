# Code of Conduct

This project helps people prepare for the German naturalisation test. Many of the people who use it and who write here are new to Germany, write in their second or third language and come from very different backgrounds. Everyone is welcome.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intentions, and ask before you assume.
- Write so that someone who is still learning the language can follow: plain words, no jokes at somebody's expense.
- Criticise the work, not the person. Say what is wrong and, if you can, how to make it better.
- Accept that the owner decides what is merged. A "no" is about the change, not about you.

## What is not acceptable

- Insults, harassment, threats or discrimination – for example because of origin, nationality, residence status, language, religion, gender, sexual orientation, age or disability.
- Publishing somebody's private information.
- Spam, advertising, and arguments about politics or migration. The issues are about the app.

## If something goes wrong

Tell the owner privately – the contact is on the [legal notice page](https://www.lebenindeutschland.org/impressum) – or use GitHub's "Report content" next to the comment.

The owner may edit or remove comments, lock or close threads, and block accounts that keep breaking these rules.

This applies to the issues, discussions and pull requests of this repository.
